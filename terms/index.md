## 2. Your PIN

The diary has its own PIN, separate from your phone's lock. That is the point
of it, and it also means:

- **We can't unlock the app for you, and we can't skip or shorten the wait.**
- If you forget your PIN, you can ask to reset it in the app. The diary then
  stays locked for **10 days** before you can set a new one. Entering your PIN
  during that time cancels the request.
- Keep your PIN somewhere you can find it. If you lose both your PIN and access
  to your account, we may not be able to help you get back in.

- **What you write is yours.** We don't claim ownership of it. You give us

- We store your diary and back up photos and voice notes nightly.
  **We don't promise that nothing will ever be lost, or that we can restore
  something that was.** Servers fail, and so do backups.
- **You can export everything, free, at any time** — Settings → Export & Import.
  You can lock the file with a password. If your diary matters to you, keep an
  export of your own.
- **Deleting your account deletes your data.** Settings → Delete account. It
  happens right away and cannot be undone. Encrypted copies of photos and voice
  notes in our backup are gone within 7 days.
- Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel that
  in iOS Settings.
- How we handle your data is in our Privacy Policy:
  [diarybff.app/privacy](https://diarybff.app/privacy/).

## 4. The AI replies

- Replies in the app are written by an AI model run by a third party

- **They are not medical, psychological, legal, financial or any other
  professional advice.** The app is not therapy and is not a crisis service.
- Replies can be wrong, can misremember what you wrote, and can miss the point.
  Don't rely on them for decisions about your health, safety, money or the law.
- **If you are thinking about hurting yourself, or you are in danger, contact
  a crisis line or emergency services now.** In the US and Canada, you can call
  or text **988**. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number, or find a line
  near you at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com). If a message on
  the chat page looks like a crisis, the app doesn't send it to the AI and
  shows you a crisis line instead.
