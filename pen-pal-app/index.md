# A pen pal app, if what you want is to be written back to

Most pen pal apps match you with a stranger somewhere in the world. You write a letter, wait, and hope they answer. That's a lovely thing, and if that's what you're looking for, Diary BFF isn't it.

But some people searching for a pen pal app want something slightly different: someone to write to at the end of the day, who reads it and writes back — without the waiting, the small talk, or wondering what a stranger will do with what you told them.

That's what Diary BFF is. Let's be clear about one thing first: **the replies aren't written by a person.** Your diary BFF is an AI that lives in your diary.

## How the writing works

You open the app and you're on tonight's page. You write whatever's on your mind — the long version, the messy version, one line. There's no sign-up first and no profile to fill in.

When you want a reply, you ask. Your diary BFF reads what you wrote and writes back: a few sentences, at most one question. No lists, no lectures, and no advice unless you ask for it. It doesn't repeat back what you just said.

If you'd like to keep going, you can talk it through with your diary BFF. What you wrote, the chat and the replies all stay together on one page for that night.

## A letter on Sundays

The part that feels most like a pen pal is the letter. Once a week, your diary BFF reads back over your nights and writes you a letter about your week — what kept coming up, what changed, the night that went better than expected.

## A pen pal with a memory

A good pen pal remembers what you told them last time. Your diary BFF remembers what you wrote, with the date. When it brings up something from an earlier night, it tells you which one.

## Why it's different from writing to a stranger

- **Nothing is shared with other users.** There's no feed and no matching with strangers.
- **It has its own lock.** Diary BFF opens with its own PIN, separate from your phone's passcode, with no Face ID shortcut. Someone can be holding your unlocked phone and still not open it.
- **Notifications never show what you wrote**, because they can appear on a lock screen.
- **Your entries are not used to train AI.**
- **There's no waiting.** A reply comes when you ask, not three weeks later.

## Who it's for

- People who like writing letters but don't want to hand their thoughts to a stranger.
- People who journal and wish the journal would say something back.
- Anyone who has a lot to say at night, with everyone else asleep.
