# A journal app with optional AI, where nothing replies until you ask

If you're looking for a journal app with optional AI, the real question is simple: does the AI wait, or does it jump in? In Diary BFF, it waits. You write, and the page stays quiet. A reply comes after you tap Ask, and not before.

## Writing comes first

Open tonight's page and type, or record a voice note. While you write, the AI doesn't comment and nothing pops up halfway through a sentence. The page stays yours until you decide otherwise.

## The reply comes after Ask

When you want a reply, tap the **Ask** button. Your diary BFF reads tonight's page and writes back right under your words: a few sentences, then it lets you get back to writing. It remembers what you wrote, with the date, and when it mentions an earlier night, it says which night.

## The first Ask asks you something back

The first time you tap Ask, the app stops and shows a screen: **Before your diary BFF reads this.** It says what gets sent: this entry, plus short notes your diary BFF kept from earlier nights, with their dates. Not your name. It says where it goes: Anthropic, the company whose model writes back, with Amazon Bedrock as a backup. Neither trains on it.

Then you choose: **Let your diary BFF read this**, or **Keep it to myself**. Say no, and in the app's own words, "the diary still works the same. It writes, it saves, it locks."

## Pick how much AI you want

- **Never ask.** Write for weeks without tapping Ask. The diary is still a diary: your writing, your voice notes, export, and its own PIN.
- **Ask on the hard nights.** Tap Ask when you want a reply. Leave it when you don't.
- **Switch it off.** In Settings, turn off **Your diary BFF writes back**, and nothing more is sent to the AI. The diary keeps everything it does on its own: writing, voice, export. Your diary BFF just stops answering.

## The Sunday letter waits for a yes too

On Sundays, your diary BFF can write you a letter about your week. It goes by the same yes as Ask. If you haven't said yes, the letter doesn't read your week; it shows **Let your diary BFF read this week** and waits.

## What stays the same either way

- The diary locks with its own PIN, separate from your phone's.
- Notifications never show what you wrote.
- Your entries are not used to train AI.
- The replies aren't written by a person; your diary BFF is an AI that lives in your diary.
