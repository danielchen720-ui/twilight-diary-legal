# A diary app for adults, built for the end of a long day

Search for a diary app for adults and a lot of what comes up is built for someone else: stickers, color themes, cartoon mascots, badges for writing three days in a row. That's fine if it's what you want. If you're a grown-up with a full day behind you and something on your mind, it can feel like the wrong room.

Diary BFF is a plain, private diary for the end of the day. It's built for people who want to write, not decorate.

## One page, no decorating

You open the app and you're on tonight's page. There are no templates to pick, no moods to rate, no stickers to place. Write the long version, the messy version, or one line. If typing is too much, hold to record and it turns into text on the same page.

There's no sign-up before you start, and no profile to fill in.

## Replies that talk to you like an adult

When you want a reply, you ask. Your diary BFF reads what you wrote and answers in a few sentences, with at most one question. No lists, no lectures, and no advice unless you ask for it. It doesn't repeat back what you just said, and it doesn't tell you what's wrong with you.

The replies aren't written by a person. Your diary BFF is an AI that lives in your diary. It remembers what you wrote, with the date, and once a week, on Sundays, it writes you a letter about your week.

## No badges, no confetti

There are no badges, no celebration animations, and no "you missed a day" messages. Skip a night, and it won't bring it up. Your diary is somewhere you go when you need it, not a habit you're being graded on.

## A lock that's actually yours

Adults share phones, couches, and households. Diary BFF opens with its own PIN, separate from your phone's passcode, with no Face ID shortcut. Someone can be holding your unlocked phone and still not open it.

- **Notifications never show what you wrote**, because they can appear on a lock screen.
- **Your entries are not used to train AI.**
- **Nothing is shared with other users.** There's no feed.

## Who it's for

- People who want a diary that looks like a page, not a game.
- People whose day only slows down late at night.
- Anyone who's kept a journal before and wished it would say something back.

## Try it tonight

Download Diary BFF, set a PIN, and write one entry tonight. Your first reply comes with the app; more replies and the full Sunday letter are part of the full version.
