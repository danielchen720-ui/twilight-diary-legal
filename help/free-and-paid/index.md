# What's free and what's in the full version

<!-- 支持回复模板 -->
**Short answer:** Writing stays free. Your first reply comes with the app; more replies and the full Sunday letter are part of the full version. Prices and any free trial are shown in the app before you start, and Apple handles the subscription.

## Free

<!-- 语音转文字不列：免费档有每日分钟上限（ai-transcribe free_transcribe_minutes_per_day），列进来会比实际宽 · paywall.freeForever「Writing stays free」· 第 25 篇 journal-prompts-app 末段已审句 · /support/ Export is free -->
- Writing, and the PIN lock.
- Export and import, as often as you like.
- Your first reply from your diary BFF.

## The full version

<!-- paywall.b1Body / b2Body / b3Body；第 25 篇已审句「more replies and the full Sunday letter」 -->
More replies and the full Sunday letter. Ask, and your diary BFF answers what you wrote — once, and short. Ask about something from last month and it brings it back, with the day you wrote it.

## Free trial

<!-- paywall.ctaTrial「Try {n} days free」· paywall.timelineToday · paywall.timelineRemind（{n}/{d} 来自商店，不写死） -->
If a free trial is offered, the button says how many days it is, and the screen shows the timeline: nothing is due on the first day, and you can turn on a reminder before it renews.

## Cancel

<!-- sub.trialHowToCancel · /support/ 已审句（24 小时是 Apple 的规则） -->
Apple handles subscriptions. Open **iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions**, pick Diary BFF, and cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before your plan renews and you won't be charged for the next period.

## Restore on a new phone

<!-- paywall.restore「Restore purchase」 -->
Signed in with the same Apple ID? Open the subscription screen and tap **Restore purchase**.
