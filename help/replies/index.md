# How your diary BFF writes back

<!-- 支持回复模板 -->
**Short answer:** Your diary BFF stays quiet while you write. Tap **Ask** and it writes back right under your words, in a few sentences. You can turn replies off in Settings and the diary keeps working the same.

## Only when you ask

<!-- journal.askShort「Ask」· paywall.b1Body「answers what you wrote — once, and short」· 第 25 篇已审句 -->
Nothing happens until you tap **Ask**. Then your diary BFF answers what you wrote — once, and short — with at most one question. It doesn't hand you a list of advice.

## It remembers, with the date

<!-- paywall.b2Body · write.remembers（第 31 篇已审写法） -->
Something you wrote last month? Ask about it and your diary BFF brings it back, and shows you the day you wrote it. **Settings → What your diary BFF remembers** lists what it keeps, and you can delete any line.

## What gets sent, and where

<!-- aiConsent.what / aiConsent.who / aiConsent.ifNot（{name} 落 your diary BFF） -->
Before the first reply, the app shows you what gets sent: that entry, plus short notes from earlier nights (with their dates). Not your name. It goes to Anthropic, the company whose model writes back; if Anthropic is down, it goes to the same model on Amazon Bedrock instead. Neither trains on it.

Say no and the diary still works the same. It writes, it saves, it locks. Your diary BFF just stays quiet.

## Turning replies off

<!-- settings.aiReplies「{Name} writes back」· settings.aiRepliesNote -->
In Settings, turn off the **writes back** switch next to your diary BFF's name. Nothing more is sent.

## A name, and he/she

<!-- companion.title · who.pronounLabel「He or she」；1.6.3 设置里没有改代词的入口 → 只说开头选，不说「随时改」 -->
When you start, the app asks what you want to call your diary BFF and lets you choose he/she. Replies and the app use the name you pick.

## It's not a person

<!-- 第 25 篇已审句 -->
The replies aren't written by a person. Your diary BFF is an AI that lives in your diary.
