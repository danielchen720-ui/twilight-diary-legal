# Delete your account

<!-- 支持回复模板 -->
**Short answer:** Go to **Settings → Delete account → Delete for good**. It happens right away and cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription; cancel that separately in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

## What gets erased

<!-- settings.deleteAccountIntro · settings.eraseEntries / eraseMedia / eraseAi / eraseAccount（逐字） -->
Once deleted, the following is erased for good:

- Every entry and everything written in it
- Voice notes and photos
- Your diary BFF's replies and Sunday letters
- The account itself

## Keep a copy first

<!-- settings.exportFirstHint · settings.exportFirstButton「Export a copy first」 -->
To keep any of it, export a copy first. The delete page has an **Export a copy first** button, so you don't have to go back. See [Export and import](../export-and-import/).

## Your subscription

<!-- settings.subscriptionNote（逐字，iOS Settings → iPhone Settings 同 /support/ 说法） -->
Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it separately in **iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions**, or you'll keep being charged.

<!-- Q207：删号时可能弹 Apple 那一张（willAskAppleOnDelete），点取消照样删 -->
If you connected an Apple ID, Apple may show one more screen while the account is deleted.
