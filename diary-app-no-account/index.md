# A diary app with no sign-up

You open a new diary app at 11pm because something's on your mind. Before you can type a word, it wants you to sign up. A form, a password, a confirmation step. By the time you're in, the thought has gone.

If you searched for a diary app with no account, that's probably why. Diary BFF is built so the first thing you do is write.

## What "no account" means in Diary BFF

There is no sign-up. No form to fill in, no password to make up, nothing to confirm. You open the app, set a PIN, and you're on tonight's page.

To be exact about it: behind the scenes, the app creates an anonymous ID so your diary has somewhere to live and can be backed up. You never see a sign-up form for it. That's why we say "no sign-up" rather than "no account" — it's the more honest way to put it.

## Why skip the sign-up

### Nothing to decide before you start

A diary is easiest to start on the night you need it, and that night is rarely a good time to make an account. With no sign-up, there are no forms to fill in and nothing to go back and confirm. You start typing.

### A lock instead of a login

Without a login, the lock is a PIN of its own — separate from your phone's passcode, with no Face ID shortcut. Someone can be holding your unlocked phone and still not open your diary.

## The trade-off, stated plainly

No sign-up also means there's no password-reset link to send. Here's what that changes:

- **If you forget your PIN**, you can start a reset with your phone's own device authentication. For your privacy, the new PIN only unlocks after a 10-day wait, so someone who knows your phone passcode can't use it to open your diary today. Your entries stay intact while you wait.
- **Keep a copy of your own.** You can export your diary any time — every entry, with your writing, the timestamps and the replies — and the backup file can have its own lock. An export is the copy that's always in your hands.

We think that trade is worth it for a diary. But it's a real trade, and it's better to know before you start.

## What you get without signing up

Diary BFF is a night diary with someone in it. You write whatever your head is doing — the conversation you keep replaying, the message you didn't send, tomorrow's list that keeps rewriting itself.

When you ask, your diary BFF reads it and says one thing back: a few sentences, at most one question, no lists and no lectures. It remembers what you wrote, with the date. On Sundays, it writes you a letter about your week.

- Notifications never show what you wrote, because they can appear on a lock screen.
- Your entries are not used to train AI.
- Skip a night, and it won't bring it up.
