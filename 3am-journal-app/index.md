# A 3am journal app, for the hour when the house goes quiet

It's 3am. The house is quiet, the phone is the only light in the room, and your mind has picked up a thought from earlier in the day and won't put it down. You could scroll. You could lie there. Or you could open a 3am journal app, write it down, and see if that helps it settle.

That's what Diary BFF is for. It's a 3am journal app in the most literal sense: something you can open in the dark, with one hand, without making a single decision first.

## Nothing to choose at 3am

You open the app and you're on tonight's page. There are no templates, no prompts you have to answer, no mood to rate. Write the long version, the messy version, or one line. If typing feels like too much, hold to record and say it; it turns into text on the same page.

## A few sentences back, if you want them

When you want a reply, you ask. Your diary BFF reads what you wrote and answers in a few sentences, with at most one question. No lists, no lectures, and no advice unless you ask for it. It doesn't repeat back what you just said.

The replies aren't written by a person. Your diary BFF is an AI that lives in your diary. It won't tell you what's wrong with you, and it isn't a replacement for talking to someone you trust.

If you want to keep going, you can talk it through. If you don't, you don't have to.

## Then put the phone down

A 3am journal app should help you close the loop, not keep you up scrolling. There's no feed and nothing to catch up on. Write it down, read the reply if you asked for one, and the page is still there tomorrow.

And if 3am turns into a regular thing, your diary BFF remembers what you wrote, with the date. On a later night it can tell you which night a thought first showed up. Once a week, on Sundays, it writes you a letter about your week.

Skip a night, and it won't bring it up.

## Private, even at 3am

- **It has its own PIN**, separate from your phone's passcode, with no Face ID shortcut.
- **Notifications never show what you wrote**, because they can appear on a lock screen.
- **Your entries are not used to train AI.**

## Who it's for

- People whose mind gets loud in the middle of the night.
- People who'd rather write than wake someone up.
- Anyone who wants the thought out of their head and onto a page, so it can wait until morning.
